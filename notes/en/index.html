Notes

A public notebook for paper-reading memos, research thoughts, and things I want to think through a little further. Mostly around NLP, AI, multilinguality, and evaluation.

How these notes are made

For a typical paper, I use GenAI as a discussion partner to question interpretations, connect related work, and explore possible follow-up ideas from my own research interests and perspective.

The discussion log is then used to generate an initial draft. These notes are not raw model outputs or neutral paper summaries; the published version reflects my own interpretation, interests, and final editorial decisions.

Ideas are open invitations

Ideas at the end of notes are open invitations. Want to test one together? Contact me. Prefer to run with it yourself? Please do — and let me know what you find.
