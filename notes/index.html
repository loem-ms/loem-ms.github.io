Notes

A public notebook for paper-reading memos, research thoughts, and things I want to think through a little further. Mostly around NLP, AI, multilinguality, and evaluation.

How these notes are made

Most notes here begin as drafts generated with GenAI from logs of my own paper-reading discussions. For a typical paper, I spend roughly 10–20 turns discussing it from my own research interests and perspective: questioning interpretations, making connections to related work, and exploring possible follow-up ideas. GenAI then helps turn that discussion into an initial draft; I review, revise, and decide what is finally published.

These notes are therefore not unreviewed model outputs, nor are they meant to be neutral summaries of the papers. GenAI is mainly a tool for discussion, drafting, and structuring; the published text reflects my own interpretation, interests, and final editorial decisions.

Ideas are open invitations

At the end of many notes, I may leave ideas or possible research directions. If one catches your interest and you would like to turn it into an experiment, project, or research collaboration, feel free to contact me. You are equally welcome to just try it yourself — and if you find something interesting, I would be very happy to hear what you learned.
